01 / Background

About & Pedagogy

Intuition-First Physics & Computational Modeling

I am a Physics educator and simulation developer based in Hyderabad, India, focused on making classical mechanics, calculus, and computational science immediately visual and manipulable. Rather than treating formulas as static symbols to memorize, my teaching centers on building mental models through direct experimentation.

My work spans interactive browser laboratories, procedural Manim animations, computer vision applications, and retrieval-augmented AI systems—integrating modern software tools into the classroom so students can probe systems, test edge cases, and see the geometry behind every law.
